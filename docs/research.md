# Research register and evidence boundaries

Accessed **30 October 2026, IST**. Sources were fetched and summarized, not copied
into this project. "Read" means written page text; embedded film clips were not
independently viewed. Book publisher pages are not the full books.

## Professional film language

| ID | Source read | What it supports | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | [Yale: Cinematography](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/cinematography/) | Framing, focus, camera angle and aspect-ratio composition | Academic instruction, not AI or audience testing |
| F2 | [Yale: Mise-en-scene](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/mise-en-scene/) | Setting, costume, light, space and performance | Contextual conventions; no universal color-to-emotion code |
| F3 | [Yale: Editing](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/editing/) | Graphic/eyeline matches, continuity, screen direction and rhythm | Descriptive film analysis, not a mandatory cut formula |
| F4 | [Yale: Sound](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/sound/) | Sound source, perspective and bridges | Does not establish a sound strategy for ten-second Reels |
| F5 | [Tom Barrance: Continuity](https://learnaboutfilm.com/film-language/sequence/) | Match on action, axis, gaze and continuous sound | Professional tutorial; metadata modified February 2025 |
| F6 | [Barrance: Composition](https://learnaboutfilm.com/film-language/picture/frame-it-right/) | Frame edges, gaze/movement room, background information | Rules of thirds are guidance, not requirements |
| F7 | [Barrance: Movement](https://learnaboutfilm.com/film-language/picture/movement/) | Static camera for important detail, planned pauses, motivated movement | Do not universalize its direction-to-emotion associations |
| F8 | [ASC: Heated Rivalry cinematography](https://theasc.com/article/heated-rivalry-cinematography/) | Jackson Parrell's practical lighting, simple blocking and deliberate palette | Production case study, April 2026; no causal emotion claim |
| F9 | [Murch: In the Blink of an Eye, publisher](https://www.silmanjamespress.com/shop/filmmaking-directing/in-the-blink-of-an-eye2nd-edition/) | Book scope: why cuts work, continuity, good-cut criteria | Publisher description only; no claimed reading of the book or verified numerical Rule of Six |
| F10 | [Film Independent: Keep Your Short Short](https://www.filmindependent.org/blog/keep-your-short-short-and-other-lessons-i-learned-during-project-involve/) | Arpita Kumar on small moments, gestures, limited secondary characters | Reflective practice on a roughly four-minute film |
| F11 | [BFI: Writing tips from the top](https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/writing-tips-from-top) | Visual alternatives to dialogue, emotional steps, fresh eyes | Practitioner guidance, not a scored virality model |

Yale credits the original guide to Mariano Prunes, Michael Raine and Mary Litch.
The newer guide's page update dates were not established. Relevant distinctions
are operationalized in the film-language and pacing playbooks.

## Experimental emotion/context evidence

| ID | Source | Design / finding | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | [Ansani et al. 2020, How Soundtracks Shape What We See](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.02242/full) | Between-subjects soundtrack conditions; N=118 and N=92; music changed several scene/character interpretations | Scene 1m55s; multiple musical dimensions varied; not every outcome significant; no Reel distribution endpoint |
| E2 | [Mobbs et al. 2006, Kuleshov effect](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1810228/) | 14 analyzed participants; contextual images changed face/mental-state attribution | Small constrained fMRI study; four-second context and 750ms faces; not a preferred shot-duration study |
| E3 | [Magliano & Zacks 2011, continuity editing and event segmentation](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/the-impact-of-continuity-editing-in-narrative-film-on-event-segme/) | University-hosted abstract: action discontinuities most associated with event boundaries | Abstract only; natural-film analysis, not randomized proof that fewer cuts improve comprehension |

- [Lighting and shadows](lighting-shadows.md): Holshevnikoff's official ARRI

  Drives required lightingPlan, shadow map, lighting skill and final review.

## What this research does not establish

- No universal optimum for hook time, film duration, shot count or reaction hold.
- No empirical scoring weights or predicted probability of going viral.
- No causal ranking between OpenArt models or native/separate audio workflows.
- No direct evidence that this project's ungenerated films alter dopamine.
- No proof YouTube examples transfer to Instagram demand or revenue.
- No current rights clearance for any particular track, likeness or upload.

The initial 10-second brief, seven-factor rubric, candidate hooks, beat timings
and choice of a separately editable pilot are **project decisions to test**.
