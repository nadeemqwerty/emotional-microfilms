## Historical completion boundary

At that earlier baseline, actual user choice, storyboard acceptance, reference permissions, budget,
generation jobs, model-output comparison, media playback, real singing, soundtrack
clearance, cold-viewer tests, final film approval and publication remain downstream
roadmap gates. The current matrix above supersedes this snapshot. No missing
approval or perception can be manufactured by setting a status flag.

The current objective does not authorize paid generation or publishing a film.
Completing the studio and its critic loop means it is ready to undertake those
steps under their real approvals, not that those future milestones are done.

## Findings not to downplay

- Creative legibility and performance, not AI polish, are the main product.
- Cost per **accepted** film includes rejected takes, stills, audio and edit time.
- Genre variety needs operational exceptions, not just a list of labels.
- A complete prompt/manifest cannot stand in for observed motion, listening,
  licensing or independent audience response.
- A replay can indicate confusion; views and watch time do not measure dopamine.
- Market interest and potential monetization remain different kinds of evidence.
- The critic loop must repair substantive contradictions and functional bugs;
  optional taste preferences need not cause endless redesign.
