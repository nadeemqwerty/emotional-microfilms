# Review, feedback and experiments

## Preproduction is not production acceptance

| Stage | Inspect | Cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| Scenario/prompt | Visible causality, intended choice, feasibility, original detail | Actual emotion, motion, sound or reach |
| Stills/animatic | Composition, continuity, initial pacing/comprehension | Final contact physics or model performance |
| Generated shots | Actual identity, motion, contact, reveal/response times | Rights or audience preference by themselves |
| Sound/final export | Actual listening, sync, transitions, phone crop/playback | Dopamine quantity or platform distribution |
| Viewer/platform results | Defined outcomes in stated conditions | Universal recipe or causal algorithm effect |

Use the shared `microfilm-review` contract and seven project-local read-only roles.
PASS always names scope/hash; missing essential evidence is BLOCKED. Review
definitions do not mean those agents have run. Human media inspection is required
if this host cannot play/hear the result.

## First-view comprehension

Before showing a film, write essential interpretation elements. Example:
Mira protects a dry seat, reveals it and offers it; Dev accepts; no payment,
reservation or coercion is implied.

Use a small convenience panel for diagnosis, e.g. 12-20 viewers across concepts,
not a powered audience-preference claim. Do not prescribe an invented industry
pass percentage. Define project acceptance criteria before the next test round.
Repeated misunderstanding of an indispensable action requires revision.

Show once at phone size, muted, without title/synopsis/emotion labels. Disable
loop if possible. Ask before replay:

1. What happened, in one sentence?
2. Who did what for whom?
3. What changed at the end, and which visible detail told you?
4. What did you feel? Free text before suggested emotion labels.
5. What was confusing, too small or too fast?
6. Would you replay or send it, and why?

Record first-view answers separately from replay answers. Distinguish enjoyment,
new detail and confusion as replay reasons. Store anonymized counts/denominators,
not private reviewer identities in Git. Fresh viewers after major revisions.

## Enjoyment is a first-class outcome

Use [the Wow & Enjoyment Analyst](entertainment-review.md) for editorial craft
feedback, separate from fresh-viewer answers. Predeclare audience/lane and
enjoyment criteria in the experiment template. After unprompted comprehension
ask what was enjoyable (including "nothing"), where boredom began, what detail/
payoff stayed with them, and why they would replay/share. Only then offer
beauty/surprise/warmth/laughter/nostalgia/awe labels; never lead with dopamine.
Record exact anonymized first-view and replay answers separately with actual
counts, version/hash, exposure conditions and limitations. No invented answers,
score threshold or audience PASS from an agent's taste. Keep primary outcome,
sample/stopping rule and windows fixed before seeing results.

## Sound and pacing experiments

See `music-brain-hooks.md` for detailed designs. Lock picture for audio-only
comparisons; randomize first exposure between foley and foley-plus-score. Arousal,
warmth, tension, pleasure and authenticity are distinct outcomes.

For pacing initially keep duration/story/footage/shot count and audio fixed; move
one boundary. If music changes too, label audiovisual pacing. Different generated
pictures plus sound compare production packages, not isolated music effects.

Predeclare primary outcome, practical improvement worth pursuing, evaluation
window and stopping rule. Do not stop at the first favorable metric. Small panels
diagnose issues; stable preference estimates require more evidence.

## Organic Instagram testing

Verify current Insights names and denominators in the account. Historical Meta
sources describe watch time including replay and Trial Reels, not the current
2026 algorithm. Availability/eligibility/settings may differ.

At predeclared windows record views/plays, reach, total/average watch time, actual
retention/completion if exposed, share events, saves, likes, comments, attributed
follows and follower/non-follower mix where available. Record duration/version,
post time, trial/auto-share setting, audio source and any paid distribution.

- Average watch time / duration is **not completion rate**.
- Views / reach is **not intentional rewatch rate**.
- Share events / reached accounts is **not the percentage of unique sharers**.
- Trial Reels and time-separated posts are not randomized A/B allocation.
- If trial auto-sharing changes audience regime, record it; keep it off during a
  planned comparison when feasible.
- Compare matched duration for audio tests; repeated concepts before generalizing.
- Report zero/unknown denominators honestly; never substitute plays for reach.

No view/share/retention threshold is justified as universal by this research.
No metric here measures dopamine.

## Cost and production learning

Count every charged still/video attempt including failures, licences and edit
minutes. Cost per accepted film uses accepted outputs as denominator; no accepted
output means no finite estimate. Reuse an accepted identity or shot only when it
actually fits, not to justify sunk costs.

Update the project rubric only from documented learning. Preserve whether an
item is craft guidance, vendor evidence, hypothesis, user preference or observed
result. Do not convert a single successful post into a neuroscience claim.
